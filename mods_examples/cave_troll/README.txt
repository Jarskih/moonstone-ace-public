Cave troll: a new monster (a troll with 60 hit points) and a new fight, put into lair 7 (the seventh lair of the map).
Install: copy creatures.ini, arenas.ini and lairs.ini into the mods drawer next to the game (PROGDIR:mods/).
Walk-through: docs/MODDING.md, "A new monster in a lair". Own pictures and sounds: the monster kit (docs/MONSTER_KIT.md).
